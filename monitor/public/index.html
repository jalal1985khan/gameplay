After a loss the next bet is sized so one win covers the open losses plus a normal win. A lower cash-out here wins more often but needs a bigger bet. A run of losses reaches your loss limit much sooner this way; it does not change the odds.

Once it is up by half the profit target, it stops for the day if the profit falls back to half of its best.

These limits decide when the autopilot stops. They cannot make a round more likely to win: every round is random, and the game keeps about 3% of all bets over time.
